# HIPAA Security Rule Evidence

## Purpose

This folder contains supporting evidence for the simulated HIPAA Security Rule risk assessment for CloudNova Health Systems.

The evidence demonstrates how administrative, physical, and technical safeguards were reviewed, security risks were identified, business associates were evaluated, and remediation actions were documented.

## Evidence Included

- HIPAA safeguard matrix
- HIPAA gap assessment
- HIPAA risk register
- Business associate review
- Security findings
- Remediation plan

## Safeguard Categories Reviewed

- Administrative Safeguards
- Physical Safeguards
- Technical Safeguards

## Key Areas

- Security Risk Analysis
- Risk Management
- Workforce Access
- Security Awareness
- Incident Response
- Contingency Planning
- Business Associate Management
- Device and Media Controls
- Access Control
- Audit Controls
- Authentication
- Transmission Security

## Final Assessment

The assessment identified High and Medium risk weaknesses affecting the protection of ePHI.

High-priority findings include risk analysis, workforce access, business associate oversight, incident response testing, and strong authentication.
